About us

About Shifa

Shifa began as a project for Asian women's wellbeing in Woking. Partners describe it as a local voluntary-sector organisation providing information, support, advice and advocacy, and it now supports ethnically diverse women more widely.

Our approach

Education

Classes and short courses that build skills and independence.

Exercise

Gentle, friendly activity to support body and mind.

Community spaces

Safe, supportive places to meet others and feel understood.

Where we work

Services run in Woking and Camberley (Surrey Heath), aswell as remotely online and by telephone.

Our story

The beginning · WokingA wellbeing project

Shifa began as a project focused on Asian women's wellbeing in Woking in 2003 and rooted in local community support.

August 2022A new home with Catalyst Support

Shifa moved to Catalyst Support. The move kept the same location and staff, providing continuity for the people already using the service.

After the Catalyst periodBecoming an independent CIC

Shifa Network became an independent Community Interest Company, continuing as a community organisation in its own right.

TodaySupporting women across Woking and Surrey

Shifa now supports ethnically diverse women more widely through information, advice, advocacy, learning, exercise and community spaces in Woking, Camberley and remotely.

Partners & community

Shifa works alongside local organisations to help women get the support they need.
